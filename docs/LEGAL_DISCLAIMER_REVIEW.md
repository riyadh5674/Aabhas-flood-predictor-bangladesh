## 1. Purpose

Document the required disclaimers, legal positioning, and public messaging for the Aabhas Flood Predictor system when deployed for use by Bangladesh government agencies and the public.

---

## 2. Core Disclaimer (Required on All Surfaces)

### 2.1 Standard Disclaimer
> **"RESEARCH PROTOTYPE — NOT AN OFFICIAL WARNING SERVICE**  
> This system is a research prototype developed for flood risk decision support. It is not an official flood warning service of the Government of Bangladesh or the Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre (FFWC). FFWC advisories and official warnings always take precedence. Predictions are probabilistic and uncertain; users should not rely solely on this system for life-safety decisions."

### 2.2 Placement Requirements
| Surface | Placement |
|---------|-----------|
| **Web app (all pages)** | Persistent banner at top, dismissible but reappears daily |
| **Station detail page** | Below station name, above prediction gauge |
| **Map page** | Bottom-left corner, always visible |
| **API responses** | `disclaimer` field in `/health`, `/predict/*`, `/alerts` |
| **Alert notifications** | Footer of every email/webhook |
| **Static site** | Same as web app |
| **Documentation** | Header of every page |

### 2.3 Language Versions
- **English:** As above
- **Bangla:** formalmente reviewed translation required
- **Bilingual:** Both languages displayed where space permits

## 5. Public Messaging Guidelines

### 5.1 Approved Terminology
| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| "Flood risk forecast" | "Flood prediction" |
| "Probability of exceedance" | "Will flood" / "Flood expected" |
| "Decision support" | "Early warning system" |
| "Model indicates" | "System predicts" |

### 5.2 Prohibited Claims
- ❌ "Accurate flood forecasting"
- ❌ "Official government warning"
- ❌ "Real-time flood monitoring" (it's near-real-time with known lags)
- ❌ "Life-safety decisions should be based on this"

### 5.3 Required Context in Communications
Every public communication must include:
1. System name: "Aabhas Flood Predictor"
2. Status: "Research prototype / Decision support tool"
3. Official authority: "FFWC (ffwc.gov.bd) is the official warning authority"
4. Uncertainty: "Probabilistic forecasts with known limitations"

## 7. Liability and Indemnification

### 7.1 Recommended Legal Position
- System provided "AS IS" without warranties
- No liability for decisions based on system output
- Users assume all risk
- Government agency indemnifies developers against third-party claims

### 7.2 Suggested Contract Clause (for agency MoU)
> "The Agency acknowledges that the Aabhas Flood Predictor is a research prototype provided for decision support purposes only. The Agency agrees not to represent the System's output as official flood warnings. The Agency assumes full responsibility for all decisions made using the System and agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Developers from any claims arising from the use of the System."
